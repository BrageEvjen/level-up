How it works

From a full calendar to one clear next step.

No lists to maintain and nothing to set up each morning. You open the app and the day is ready.

Connect your calendar

Sign in and let Level Up read your Google Calendar. Tell the coach in a sentence who you are and when your day starts and ends.

Get today's plan

Your coach builds the day around your lectures, meetings and deadlines, and puts the hard things where you focus best.

Do one quest at a time

The app shows a single quest. Start it, finish it, collect the XP and move on to the next one.

Day planning

A plan built around your real day.

Appointments stay where they are. Quests fill the gaps between them, sized for the time you actually have.

  • Reads your calendar, so the plan never collides with a lecture or the dentist.
  • Rough day? Tell the coach and the whole plan gets lighter.
  • Big deadline? Split it into steps that show up as quests over the week.

Progress

Level up for getting things done.

Every quest is worth XP. Show up day after day and your streak grows. The numbers are small, honest and yours.

  • XP and levels for every quest you finish.
  • A streak that counts the days you won.
  • Your week at a glance, with the last five weeks beside it.

Bosses and achievements

One boss to beat every week.

Each week gets one big goal taken from what is going on in your life: the exam on Friday, the thesis draft, three job applications. Beat it for bonus XP.

  • Weekly boss chosen from your own goals and deadlines.
  • Achievements for first steps, full weeks and long streaks.
  • Secret achievements when your coach sees something worth a medal.

A coach, not a to-do list

It gets to know you.

Level Up notices when you focus best, which quests you actually finish and how long things take. Tomorrow's plan is built on that. You can also just tell it things, and it remembers.

  • Focuses best before lunch
  • Statistics exam on Friday
  • A rough day means one small win
  • Trains every Wednesday at 17:00

Questions

Good to know.

When can I start using Level Up?

Level Up is in its last stretch before opening on the web, and it will work in the browser on your phone and computer. Apps for iPhone and Android follow. Send an e-mail to brage.net@gmail.com and you will hear from us when it opens.

What does the app need access to?

Your Google Calendar, and only with your permission. Level Up reads your events to plan the day. It adds or deletes an event only when you ask for it, and deleting always needs your approval in the app. You can withdraw the permission at any time.

How do the 7 free days work?

A day counts when you use the app to plan it. Days you do not open the app are not used up, so a busy week does not eat your trial. You do not enter a card to start.

What happens when the free days are used?

The AI coach pauses until you subscribe. Your quests, XP, streak and level stay where they are, and you are never charged without choosing a plan yourself.

How do I cancel, and can I get a refund?

You cancel in the app under Settings, Account, whenever you like, and keep access until the end of the month you paid for. If you are not happy, write to brage.net@gmail.com within 14 days of a charge and you get that charge back in full. The details are in the terms.

What happens to my data?

Your plans, quests and progress are stored so the app can work, and the parts needed to plan your day are sent to an AI model. Your data is not sold, there is no advertising, and you can download or delete everything. Read the privacy policy.

Who is behind Level Up?

Level Up is made and sold by Brage Evjen, an independent developer in Norway. Questions go straight to him at brage.net@gmail.com.
