Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Level Up plans your day, so it has to know a few things about your day. This page says what is stored, why, and who else touches it. The short version: your data is used to run the app for you, it is never sold, and there is no advertising or tracking.

Who is responsible

Brage Evjen, a private individual in Norway, is responsible for your data (the data controller).

Contact about privacy: brage.net@gmail.com

This website

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics or trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when the page is loaded. If you send us an e-mail, your address and message are kept so we can answer you.

What the app stores

  • Your account: your e-mail address and, when you sign in with Google, Google's id for your account.
  • What you make in the app: your plans and quests, projects, what you ask the coach to remember, your settings, the text under "About me", and the day's chat.
  • Your progress: XP, level, streak and achievements.
  • If you subscribe: Stripe's id for you as a customer and the state of your subscription. Card details are held by Stripe and never reach us.
  • Use of the coach: how many times a day you have used it, to keep use within fair limits.

Your calendar

With your permission the app reads events from your Google Calendar to plan your day, and it can add or delete events when you ask it to. Deleting always needs your approval in the app. Calendar events are fetched when they are needed and are not kept on our server, apart from what ends up in the plans and chat listed above. The key that lets the app reach your calendar is stored encrypted. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your Google account.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The AI coach

To plan your day and answer in the chat, the app sends the relevant parts of the data above, for example today's calendar events, your quests and your "About me" text, to an AI model run by a cloud provider on our behalf. The model is used only to produce your plan and answers. Your data is not used to train AI models.

Who else handles data

A few companies process data for us so the service can run: a cloud hosting provider (the app and its database), Google (sign-in and calendar), an AI model provider (the coach) and Stripe (payment). They may only use the data to deliver their part of the service. Some of them may process data outside the EEA; when they do, it is covered by the EU's standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard. A list of the current providers is available on request.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Why we are allowed to

We process your data to deliver the service you have asked for (the agreement with you), to read your calendar when you have said yes (your consent), and to keep records of payments as bookkeeping rules require (legal obligation).

How long

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is deleted, and backups are overwritten within 30 days. Records of payments are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

In the app, under Settings, Account, you can download everything stored about you and delete your account with all its data. You can also ask us for access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data at brage.net@gmail.com, and you can withdraw a consent at any time. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or to the authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters to you, you are told in the app or by e-mail before the change applies.
